# Real-device smoke test — PLAYER_OS_BETA_RC1

10–15 minutes on a real phone. Do it once on iPhone/Safari and once on
Android/Chrome if possible. Record results in the table at the bottom.
Rule: if you have to pinch-zoom to do ANY step, that step is a FAIL.

Use https://mido11.com — not the vercel.app URL.

1. **Login** — sign in. Kill the tab, reopen mido11.com: still signed in?
2. **Locker** — is it obvious what MIDO wants you to do next (NBA card)?
   Tap it. Tap browser BACK — do you land back on the Locker sanely?
3. **Study** — open Study, start one, type a note ON THE PHONE KEYBOARD:
   does the keyboard cover the input or the save button?
4. **Training** — Draft with MIDO → accept → open the session runner.
   Readable at arm's length? Complete a block.
5. **Film** — open your highlight. Play, pause, seek. Rotate to
   landscape and back: controls still fine?
6. **Draw** — pause, tap Draw. Draw an arrow with your finger — does the
   line appear exactly under your finger? Rotate the phone, draw again —
   still under your finger? Tap a colour dot (easy to hit?). Save the
   drawing.
7. **Download** — Save image → does the phone actually save/offer the
   file? Open it: sharp, complete?
8. **Vision** — run a Quick read on 20–30s. Does the identity line
   ("Reading you as…") make sense? Is the result honest about who you are?
9. **Community** — open Community, tap Create (the floating + — does it
   sit clear of the bottom of the screen?), attach a photo FROM YOUR
   CAMERA ROLL, post it. Then delete it from the ⋯ menu.
10. **Share** — open Publish, pick a card, change the accent, Download.
    If the native share sheet appears on Share, does it work?
11. **Profile photo** — Settings → Add a picture → camera roll → crop
    result appears in the topbar and on your posts?
12. **Orientation + back** — rotate on 2–3 pages; use browser back
    through the whole journey. Anything trapped or lost?
