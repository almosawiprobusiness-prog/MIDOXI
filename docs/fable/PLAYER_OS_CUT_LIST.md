The original recommendations follow, kept for the reasoning.

**Date:** 25 August 2026 · **Rule:** nothing here has been deleted.
These are recommendations, each with the reason and the cost of keeping
it. The bar a feature must clear is the product's own: does it serve
"MIDO knows where I am, where I want to go, and what to do next"?

The Player OS navigation currently exposes **18 destinations** (6 Work +
7 More + 5 in the command palette's tail). The core loop — Locker,
Matches, Film Room, Training, Development, Study — touches six. Every
extra destination spends a little of the player's belief that everything
in this product matters.

---

## Recommend cutting from Player OS navigation

### 1 · Meetings
A scheduling surface for club staff, surfaced to a player who will
almost never originate a meeting. It duplicates Calendar for the one
case (an event with other people) the Calendar already renders.
**Cost of keeping:** one more "why is this here?" on every player's
first week.
**Recommendation:** remove from Player OS nav. Keep the route for
coach-shared links. If meetings matter to players later, they arrive as
calendar entries, not as a section.

### 2 · Community
A feed in a product whose whole thesis is *your own record, read
properly*. It is the one section with no connection to the development
loop — nothing in it feeds a signal, a recommendation, or a goal — and
community products die without critical mass, which 11 founding players
cannot provide. A half-empty feed makes the whole product feel quieter.
**Recommendation:** remove from Player OS nav for beta. Revisit when
there are enough players for it to be alive on day one.

### 3 · Referrals as a nav destination
"Refer" is a growth mechanic, not a football feature, and it sits in the
same list as Development. **Recommendation:** move to
Settings/Membership. The feature survives; the nav slot does not.

---

## Recommend demoting or merging

### 4 · Performance vs Matches overlap
Both pages render the match list, season totals, and form. Performance's
unique value is per-90s, workload, and honesty labels.
**Recommendation:** keep both for beta (Performance's honesty framing is
a differentiator) but treat "merge Performance into Match Center" as an
open question for after real usage data. If players visit one and not
the other, merge them.

### 5 · Assessments (if surfaced to players)
Coach-side capability. A player self-assessment without a coach's
counter-signature is a rating MIDO otherwise refuses to invent.
**Recommendation:** confirm it is coach-gated; do not surface in Player
OS.

---

## Keep, with conviction — things a cut pass might wrongly kill

- **Memory** — now visibly feeds the Locker ("You told MIDO…"). This is
  the product's thesis made tangible. Keep in nav.
- **Timeline** — with goal-threads it is the development story. Keep.
- **Recovery** — feeds readiness, which gates training recommendations.
  Load-bearing, not a wellness add-on.
- **The Briefing** — coexists with Next Best Action via the overlap map;
  the two together read as one intelligence. Keep both engines.
- **Knowledge check on studies** — looks like a school quiz at first
  glance, but it is the only place the product verifies that studying
  happened rather than scrolling. Keep.

---

## Not features, but worth cutting anyway

- **"Drafting with MIDO comes with…" paywall banner on Match Center** —
  it renders above the fold on a page whose free function is complete.
  Move upsell copy below the player's own record. The record is theirs;
  the upsell is ours.
- **The second "Log a match" CTA** in the top bar when the Locker's
  Quick Entry already carries it — harmless on desktop, crowding on
  mobile. Review at the next mobile pass.
